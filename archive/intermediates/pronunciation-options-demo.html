Pronunciation Options — Dummy Demo

All four approaches on the same word. Pick whichever you like.
Option 1Built-in browser voice (Web Speech API)
No files, no internet needed once voices are cached. Uses your device's installed Italian voice. Quality varies by device.
il bagno
Click “Speak” to hear the built-in Italian voice.
Option 2External pronunciation site (Forvo / Wiktionary links)
Real human recordings from Forvo and a link to the Wiktionary entry. Needs internet; opens in a new tab.
il bagno Forvo ↗ Wiktionary ↗
Links update automatically with the selected word.
Option 3Embedded audio file (MP3 per word)
Pre-recorded audio bundled with the app — best fidelity, works fully offline. Requires generating one MP3 per card (~hundreds of files). This demo plays a sample clip generated for the current word via a TTS service.
il bagno
Demo: this plays a freshly generated MP3 for the current word (stands in for a bundled file).
Option 4TTS fetched at render time (Google Translate TTS)
When each card is shown, the app fetches a short MP3 from Google Translate's free TTS endpoint. No local files; needs internet per play. Unofficial endpoint — could change someday.
il bagno
Click “Play TTS” to fetch and play the clip for the current word.
