Notes:
- Chapters 6, 12, and 18 are review chapters with no new vocabulary section.
- The book continues to Chapter 41 ("Viaggiando per l'Italia"), a final
  review/culmination with no standard vocab list.

Each file preserves the book's own category structure (nomi / aggettivi / verbi /
avverbi e altro) as printed. Words are listed with article where applicable,
followed by the English gloss. Stress marks appear as capital letters in the
original (e.g. `la tAvola`) and have been left as-is to match the source.

These files are source material for future flashcard expansion — they have not
yet been incorporated into the flashcard app (`ims-flashcards*.html`).
